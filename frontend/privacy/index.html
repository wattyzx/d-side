Privacy & cookies

Draft · Updated 23 October 2026

Private beta — invited testers only. D-side is still being developed. This privacy notice is being finalised. For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, email us.

What we use and why

When you connect Discogs, we receive your username, access tokens, collection and wantlist. We use these, your seller searches and filters to recommend records and make wantlist updates you request. Connecting is optional, but these features need your account data. We do not receive your Discogs password or take payments.

We also fetch seller and listing information from Discogs. Our current cache stores full API responses, which can include notes, ratings, dates added, collection identifiers, seller statistics and shipping/payment-policy text.

Who receives data

Render hosts D-side and Turso stores its database, both configured in EU regions. Discogs handles account authorisation and API requests. Artwork hosts receive image requests; YouTube receives browser information when previews load. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Authorised operators may access data to run the service or handle requests. Data may be disclosed where legally required. EU hosting does not exclude processing outside the EEA by providers or connected services.

How long data stays

By default, pending logins expire after 10 minutes, sessions and stored access credentials after 7 days, and cached Discogs responses after 6 hours. Expired database records are removed by periodic cleanup while the service runs, not necessarily immediately. Access credentials are encrypted in the database.

Signing out deletes the current session and this tab’s saved search. It does not erase all server data, other-device sessions or your Discogs account. Username-bearing cache metadata currently has no automatic expiry. Some preference counts remain in working memory until replaced or the process restarts.

Clicking a playable cover first shows a choice. YouTube's player and scripts load only after you select Allow YouTube and play. YouTube then receives browser information, including your IP address, and may use cookies or other identifiers. This is optional: choose Not now to dismiss the preview, or use the external YouTube link to visit YouTube instead. See Google's privacy policy.

Your choice applies to previews on the current page only and is not saved across reloads. Select Withdraw YouTube consent in the app footer to stop playback and reload the page, unloading YouTube's player and scripts. Further previews require a new choice. Closing the player alone does not withdraw consent. Withdrawal cannot undo data already sent or erase cookies already set by YouTube; these can be managed through your browser and Google settings. Provider storage types and durations still need verification.

Your rights

Contact us using the email below to request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, subject to GDPR conditions. You may object to legitimate-interest processing, including related profiling, and withdraw consent where used without affecting earlier lawful processing. These rights also apply to sellers whose personal data we process.

Requests are generally free and answered without undue delay, normally within one month. A permitted extension of up to two further months must be explained within the first month. We may verify identity where reasonably needed. Never send passwords or access tokens.

You can revoke D-side’s access in Discogs settings; this does not erase stored data. You may complain to your supervisory authority, including Italy’s Garante.

Request deletion of your data

Email us with the subject D-side data deletion request and your Discogs username. You can request deletion of personal data held by D-side, including stored access credentials, sessions and account-specific caches. Sellers can also contact us about their personal data.

This is a manual request, not automatic deletion. We may ask for proportionate verification of your identity; never send your password or access tokens. We will respond without undue delay, normally within one month, and explain any lawful retention or applicable extension. Deletion from D-side does not delete your Discogs account, collection or wantlist. Sign out to clear the saved search in your current browser tab; other tabs or devices may retain their own browser data.
